PRIVATE PERSONAL PILOT

Jarvis

A personal assistant for checking your mail and calendar through explicit, bounded requests.

What the app does

Jarvis runs in its owner's environment. It can search recent Gmail messages, show requested message details and read upcoming events from the connected account's primary Google Calendar. A Telegram interface provides access for its paired owner.

Personal and work accounts are kept separate. When both are connected, each read request must explicitly select the intended account.

Google account access

Google sign-in confirms which account is connected. Read permissions allow Jarvis to retrieve mail and calendar information only for the features described here. Encrypted credentials let an approved connection resume after restarting the app.

Separate, explicitly enabled flows may create calendar events after an approval preview, or read a Drive file selected by the user. Those permissions are not part of the persistent mail/calendar read connection.

A private app, not a public service

Jarvis is an experimental personal project. This website describes the app; it does not provide registration, Google sign-in or access to anyone's connected accounts. Jarvis is not affiliated with Google or Telegram.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy policy before connecting an account. For connection, access or deletion questions, use the developer support contact displayed on the Google consent screen or contact the operator who provided your installation.
