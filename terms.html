Terms of use

Jarvis is an experimental personal assistant operated in its owner's environment. It is not a public subscription service and this website offers no account registration or purchases.

Authorized accounts and actions

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to use. Work accounts remain subject to their organization's access rules. Select the intended personal or work account for each request. Review any proposed external action, including recipients and event details, before approving it.

Experimental operation

Availability and results may be affected by local configuration, network failures, provider limits, expired or revoked permissions, and software defects. Check important information against its original source. The pilot does not provide a service-level guarantee.

Privacy and stopping use

Read the privacy policy for data handling, retention, external services and deletion controls. You can stop the local runtime and revoke Google access at any time. Contact the installation's operator or the support contact shown on the Google consent screen for assistance.

Independent project

Jarvis is not affiliated with Google or Telegram. Their services are subject to their respective terms and policies. These pilot usage notes do not restrict rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
